# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus on technical issues and collaboration.
- Accept feedback in good faith.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Doxxing, intimidation, or threats.
- Sharing private or sensitive information without consent.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate this code of conduct.
